Fun with functions on spheres

Three examples of functions S² → ℝ, exploring different visualization strategies

🌊 SH Flow

Smooth & signed (toy example): Infinitely differentiable function defined by spherical harmonic coefficients. Values range freely over positive and negative reals.

Smooth • Signed

📊 ETOPO Range

Jagged & signed (Earth elevation): Piecewise constant on HEALPix cells. Each cell maps to elevation range [min, max] in meters, spanning negative (below sea level) to positive values.

Jagged • Signed

🌍 World Population

Jagged & nonnegative: Piecewise constant on HEALPix cells. Each cell maps to population count—strictly positive integers representing people per region.

Jagged • Counts
